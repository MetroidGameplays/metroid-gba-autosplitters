# English

🚀 I'm sharing a preview of my autosplitters for **Metroid: Zero Mission (USA)** and **Metroid Fusion (JPN)**!

These are **LiveSplit** `.asl` scripts tested in **BizHawk with the mGBA core**, featuring automatic starts, configurable checkpoints, and settings in **Portuguese and English**.

Confirmed in the emulator so far:

- **Zero Mission:** starting when A confirms difficulty, and the Morph Ball split.
- **Fusion JPN:** Auto-Start, the Missiles download, and Morph Ball absorption.

The project is still being tested: the remaining checkpoints and final run split need validation. This preview defaults to RTA and targets emulation; NSO support and integrated time conversion are planned for a later stage.

Files and setup instructions are available in this repository. If you try it and find a problem, please open an Issue with your ROM region, BizHawk version, and script log. Feedback and testing are welcome! 🎮
